Pursuing BTech in Electrical Engineering with a major in E-Vehicle Technology at Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (_An Institution of National Importance, Govt. of India_).

From Prayagraj, India.

My interests drift through history, geography, lores and architecture, guided by a quiet obsession with processes - how things are built, shaped and endure. Raised on newspapers rather than noise, I read cities like palimpsests, noticing their smallest details. I seek forgotten monuments, silent streets and offbeat histories, choosing layered stone and memory over passing crowds.
