I'm an engineering undergraduate at the Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology - Bengaluru Campus, India.

I recently completed a Summer Internship at Reliance Industries Limited – Vadodara Manufacturing Division, where I worked on large-scale industrial power systems and electrical infrastructure, including power distribution, system architecture, and industrial power-system applications.

I have worked on projects spanning AI applications, computer vision and data-driven systems, actively participated and won in national hackathons like HMEL QUEST 2025, SIH 2024 and innovation challenges, and have co-authored few research papers.
Beyond above, I actively contribute to the leadership and community initiatives. I served as the Secretary of the Cultural Council of RGIPT and previously contributed as the Overall Coordinator of the 1st Edition of URJA SANGAM - 2025, the Annual Festival of RGIPT. I have also contributed as Joint Secretary of the IEEE RGIPT Student Branch and as a Student Training & Placement Coordinator for my department.

I enjoy exploring heritage sites, studying local folklore and regional histories, reading history and literature, appreciating Indian classical music, swimming and watching films.
